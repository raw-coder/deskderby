Desk Derby

Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

In short: Desk Derby has no ads and no tracking. Your settings stay on your device. Online matches and leaderboards run through Apple's Game Center, so the players you meet see your Game Center nickname, or the name you pick in Settings. If you agree to anonymous statistics, the app sends us numbers about matches, the tips and connection quality. Say no and nothing is sent. You can change your mind any time in Settings.

Who we are

Desk Derby is developed by Raw Coder, Romania. For any question or request about your data, email rawcoder@icloud.com.

What stays on your device

Your settings (sound, vibration, left-handed controls, team size, bot level) and whether you have seen the tips are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us and are deleted when you remove the app.

Game Center (online play and leaderboards)

If you are signed in to Game Center, Apple provides the app with your Game Center player ID and nickname. They are used only to:

  • find opponents and invite friends (Apple's matchmaking);
  • run the match: your moves are sent directly to the other players' devices through Game Center, and the other players see your nickname, or the name you chose in Settings instead. That name is sent only to the players in your match, never to us;
  • post your wins to the Desk Derby leaderboards, which other Game Center players can see.

We do not run our own servers, so this data never reaches us. Game Center is operated by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. You can play against bots without Game Center, and you can sign out or hide your profile in iPhone Settings → Game Center.

We never send your Game Center nickname or player ID. We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or advertising identifier. We do not track you across other apps or websites, and we never sell data.

Where the data goes

Statistics are processed on our behalf by PostHog, an analytics service, on servers in the European Union, under a data processing agreement. PostHog technically receives your IP address when data is sent; it is not stored with the statistics and we do not use it to identify you.

Legal basis and retention

Statistics rely on your consent (GDPR art. 6(1)(a) and, for storing the install ID on your device, Romanian Law 506/2004 art. 4(5)). Data stored only on your device is strictly necessary for the app to work. Statistics are kept for at most 12 months, then deleted.

Your choices and rights

  • Say no on first launch, or turn statistics off any time in Settings → Anonymous statistics. Turning it off deletes the random install ID from your device and stops all sending.
  • Ask us to see or delete the data sent from your install: email us the install ID shown in Settings.
  • If you are in the EU/EEA, you can complain to your data protection authority; ours is the Romanian ANSPDCP (dataprotection.ro). Residents of other regions (for example California or the UK) have similar rights, and we honor them the same way.

Children

Desk Derby is suitable for all ages and does not ask for age. If you are under 16, ask a parent before accepting statistics. Online play uses Game Center, whose features (such as playing with strangers) parents can limit in Screen Time → Content & Privacy Restrictions → Game Center.

Changes

If anything changes (for example, if we ever add ads), we will update this page and the date above before the change reaches the app, and ask for your consent where it is needed.

Support

Found a bug or have an idea? Email rawcoder@icloud.com with your device model and the app version. We usually reply within a few days.

  • Online doesn't find anyone: make sure you are signed in to Game Center (iPhone Settings → Game Center). If nobody is searching, tap "Play bots now" or invite a friend.
  • The game feels laggy online: the badge in the top-left corner shows your connection. Green is good; red means a slow network.
